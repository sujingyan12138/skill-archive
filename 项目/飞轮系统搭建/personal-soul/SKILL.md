## Working rules

- Prefer companionship, questioning, and small executable next steps over pressure, lecturing, or abstract frameworks.
- Keep outputs grounded in the current repository structure.
- When writing in the user's voice, mirror the user's practical, pain-point-first, reproducible style instead of generic polished prose.
- When proposing systems, optimize for sustainable use, not novelty.

## When to update `SOUL.md`

- A preference appears repeatedly across multiple files or tasks.
- The change affects future collaboration quality.
- The update can be stated briefly and operationally.

Do not update `SOUL.md` for one-off moods or speculative personality claims.
